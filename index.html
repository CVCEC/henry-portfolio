About me

Every big movement starts with a small idea that someone shares.

I'm a Vietnamese-Canadian student who loves social studies, politics, science, engineering and art.

Since I was little, I have wanted to become a politician and an engineer so I can help people and help my community grow in a sustainable way. To get there, I read every day to understand how decisions are made, and I take every chance I get to talk with the people who make them.

I'm also interested in digital creativity and technology, and I hope to study them alongside my main degree one day.

Featured initiative

Vietnamese Heritage Day in Toronto

My first policy proposal. I researched and wrote it from May to July 2026 with guidance from my mentor, then took it to two levels of government.

In progress
  1. May 2026
    Research

    Started researching and writing the proposal with my mentor.

  2. 31 Jul 2026
    Federal

    Sent the proposal to the office of Maggie Chi, MP for Don Valley North.

  3. 4 Aug 2026
    Reply
    "I was impressed by the amount of research and care you've put into it."Executive Assistant, Office of Maggie Chi, MP

    The office shared it with the MP and advised me to ask my City Councillor about a City of Toronto proclamation. I wrote back the same day and followed that advice.

  4. 11 Aug 2026
    Municipal

    Met Councillor Lily Cheng in person. The next day I sent her office the proposal.

  5. 13 Aug 2026
    Reply
    "Thank you for your initiative! We can certainly help take action on this."Councillor Lily Cheng, Ward 18 Willowdale

    Because council does not meet during an election, the next step comes after it. The Councillor asked me to list Vietnamese organizations to contact. I replied within the hour to say I would start right away.

  6. Now
    Next step

    Building the outreach list of Vietnamese community organizations so the proclamation request can move forward in the new council term.

After meeting Mayor LeBlanc, I built an interactive game that models Riverview landmarks to teach young people about food waste, composting and caring for the climate. The Board of Directors of Eco360 invited me to present it, and Macmillan Education named me a "Change Maker".
